# VIGIA

**Security-risk triage and remediation planning for small development teams without a dedicated security function.**

VIGIA began as an academic project and evolved into a product hypothesis: small teams often receive security findings or infrastructure signals but lack a dedicated person to prioritize what matters first.

## Problem

A list of vulnerabilities is not a remediation strategy. Teams need context, prioritization and a clear path from finding to action.

## Product direction

VIGIA is designed to:

- ingest security findings and infrastructure reports;
- normalize and classify them;
- prioritize risk based on severity and context;
- organize remediation work;
- keep the reasoning behind priority visible;
- turn findings into an actionable correction plan.

## Engineering focus

**Application security · threat intelligence · prioritization · Supabase · product engineering**

VIGIA is not positioned as a replacement for a security team, penetration test or professional incident response. Its role is to give smaller engineering teams a structured security baseline and help them act on findings more consistently.
